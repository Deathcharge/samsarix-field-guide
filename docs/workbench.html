    05 / Read the recommendation correctly

    Fit is not quality, security, or readiness.

    What the workbench ranks

    Declared use-case fit, then how many selected constraints have an evidence-backed evaluation path.

    What it refuses to score

    Security, maintenance, package publication, compatibility, or production readiness without destination-owned evidence.

    What leaves your browser

    Nothing until you open a repository. The brief lives in the URL; there is no account, analytics, storage, or background request.
